Tackling

The defense missed a historic number of tackles. Is it technique, effort, or personnel, and what changes before Sunday?

28missed tackles at Kansas City

Evidence

  • Miss rate 29.8%, third-worst in an NFL game since 2018; 247 yards allowed after missed tackles, the most in that span (Next Gen Stats).
  • Kenneth Walker III: 173 rushing yards, 144 after contact.
  • Seven defenders with multiple misses: Singleton, Strnad, Surtain, Moss, McMillian, Hufanga, Brandon Jones. Zach Allen was one of two starters with none.

What the staff has said

Payton: “We will look at the technique.” Allen: “It’s very correctable … it’ll get addressed, it’ll get fixed.” No practice, personnel or scheme change reported as of this page.

Watching for

Any stated practice change (live tackling periods), a linebacker or safety rotation, and the missed-tackle count against Jacksonville.

Sources: denver7.com, Sept 15 2026; si.com Mile High Huddle, Sept 15 2026; both citing Next Gen Stats.

Third-down play calling: not running

On third down the offense threw almost every time, including on third and 1. With a new play-caller, is the run being abandoned when it is the higher-percentage call?

1 of 12third downs with a designed run

Evidence

  • Third-down conversions 2 of 12. Of the twelve real third-down snaps: one designed run, one scramble, ten pass calls.
  • The one designed run, third and 1 in the first quarter, gained 6 and converted. The other third and 1 was a pass, incomplete. A third and 2 in the third quarter was lost to a delay-of-game penalty.
  • Whole game: 15 rushes for 61 yards against Kansas City’s 38 for 220; time of possession 26:18 to 33:42.

What the staff has said

Davis Webb, offensive coordinator, called the plays; Payton oversees, an arrangement announced in February. Payton after the game: “I didn’t like the time of possession and how that game began.”

Watching for

Third-down run rate in short yardage against Jacksonville, and whether Payton takes any part of the call sheet back.

Sources: ESPN box score and play-by-play, Broncos at Chiefs, Sept 14 2026; gazette.com, Sept 13 2026; si.com, Sept 15 2026.
