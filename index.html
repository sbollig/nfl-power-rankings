Update rule, mine

Todd's ratings never move on their own. After each week I shift both teams by one fifth of the surprise, the actual home margin minus Todd's projected margin, clamped to ±21 points. The home team gains that amount and the away team loses it, so the league total never drifts. Broncos and Chiefs are untouched until tonight's final.
